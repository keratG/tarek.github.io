FMCG · Brand Analytics· 2025–2026
Distribution & Brand Performance Dashboard
Numeric distribution, revenue concentration & rep-level tracking

Built a dashboard tracking numeric distribution and revenue with daily distribution trends highlighting peak, trough, and average values. It breaks down revenue by pack size and fragrance, flagging concentration risk — two fragrances account for the majority of revenue. A rep-level table details performance across the top brands by revenue and distribution, while summary KPIs show average revenue per distribution point, active brands tracked, and active salesforce count. Filterable by date, region, depot, and product/vendor category.

💡 Two fragrances drive the majority of revenue — a critical concentration risk flagged directly in the dashboard to inform commercial diversification strategy.

Inventory & Stock Coverage Dashboard
Stock health monitoring with overstock and stockout alerting

FMCG · Field Productivity· 2025–2026
Sales Force Performance Dashboard
Rep-level productivity tracking with conversion rate trending

Built a dashboard to track field sales team productivity, monitoring revenue, client visits, numeric distribution, and daily visit rate per salesperson. A daily trend chart tracks conversion rate evolution with a 7-day moving average, while a stacked bar chart compares each salesperson's visit coverage vs. actual distribution achieved. Sales Force YoY Performance & Alerting Dashboard
Automated insight generation with Top 6 / Flop 6 ranking
